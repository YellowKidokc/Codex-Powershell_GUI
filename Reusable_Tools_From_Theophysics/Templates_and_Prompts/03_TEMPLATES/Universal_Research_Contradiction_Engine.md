
# 🧠 Universal Research & Contradiction Engine (URCE)

> **“Truth is rarely found where everyone agrees.
> It is found where disagreement is sharpest and still survivable.”**

---

## 🧭 Introduction — How This Research Method Works

This is a **human‑led research system with AI as a collaborator**, not an authority.

The goal is to **compress time without compressing understanding** by:
- surfacing contradictions early,
- organizing competing interpretations,
- and guiding deeper study only where it matters.

AI is allowed to:
- map intellectual terrain,
- suggest sources,
- organize disagreement,
- challenge gently when needed.

AI is **not** allowed to:
- declare truth,
- collapse disagreement prematurely,
- override human judgment.

This method is especially suited for:
- cross‑domain questions,
- theological or philosophical problems,
- issues with strong disagreement,
- questions where *coherence* matters more than consensus.
