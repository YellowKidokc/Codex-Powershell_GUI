# PROVENIS — Trace It Back to Where It Actually Started

> You are not writing a bibliography. You are following the structural DNA backward until you find the organism that produced it.

You think you know where your ideas come from. You're almost certainly wrong. The idea you had in the shower didn't start in the shower. The breakthrough in your research didn't start with the paper you were reading. PROVENIS forces you to stop accepting surface origins and trace the actual chain — which is always longer, stranger, and more revealing than the story you tell yourself.

## 1. THE SURFACE ORIGIN

What's the obvious story? State it plainly.

That's the press release. Now kill it.

## 2. THE ACTUAL CHAIN

Trace backward. Each node must connect to the previous by structural logic, not just chronology.

**Node 4 (immediate):** Gemini proposed 20 templates → Claude reduced to 8 → GPT confirmed orthogonality. The convergence happened in a single evening.

**Node 2 (a year prior):** David asked AI systems to identify the ten most fundamental laws binding physicists. That question IS the compression instinct. The eight operators exist because the same person who compressed physics into ten variables would inevitably compress creation into a small operator set.

**Node 1 (the real floor):** David doesn't think in topics. He thinks in systems. Content creators organize by subject. Scientists organize by methodology. David organizes by structural function — "what does this note DO?" The eight operators are not an invention. They are a transcription of how one mind already worked, made legible for the first time.

The surface says: "AIs designed templates."

That changes everything. It means the eight operators aren't arbitrary design choices — they're empirical observations of how structured thinking already works in at least one confirmed case. The AIs didn't create them. They excavated them.
